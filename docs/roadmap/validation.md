# Validation and Acceptance Plan

## Authority and claim rule

This document is the sole normative owner of support claims and required evidence. It proves public requirements in [Product contract](product-contract.md) and invariants in [Architecture](architecture.md); it does not redefine either. [Delivery plan](delivery-plan.md) owns when a row gates implementation or release.

All `VAL-*` rows are fixed Phase 1 evidence requirements; their required observations are unchanged by delivery's split gate. A finite positive row may be `failed`, `inconclusive`, or `implementation-qualified`; a finite negative row may be `failed`, `inconclusive`, or `exclusion-proven`. None is a support state. Changing a row or candidate requires the owning contract edit, affected evidence rerun, and applicable review. A Linux/platform/filesystem tuple, public native Git identity, shell, mise declaration/backend/package class, chezmoi entry/template behavior, or parser behavior is **unsupported** until VAL-001 and its complete Phase 6 installed-binary vertical pass. Selected gitlinks, private transport/authentication, provider-backed rendering, and host-fact-dependent behavior are exclusions and can never become support through an MVP proof row.

Cloud conformance is local source validation, not a new `VAL-*` qualification row. Its current scope is disposable `./internal/proof/...` race checks, all-package vet/configured lint, read-only module verification, an unchanged-old-entrypoint compile and cloud-script boundary tests. A source-bound report must distinguish `passed`, `failed` and `not-exercised`; inventory-only mode and missing required local checks cannot pass the cloud gate. Kernel/distribution/filesystem/tool/CA inventory records observed differences without running host-qualification probes, and `host_qualified=false` and `release_supported=false` remain unconditional. Even matching inventory cannot qualify any candidate; missing real-host rows remain not exercised and the candidate dispositions above remain inconclusive. Future replacement implementation additionally requires focused permanent replacement tests/race checks and its phase-specific reviews. Mock/local refusal results never replace the mandatory cgroup, namespace, btrfs, exact-tool/CA or installed-binary matrix.
